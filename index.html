How we choose our picks

We don’t have a lab or a test team. Instead, we spend months reading through owner reviews on major hunting forums, retailer sites, and social media groups. We look for patterns: which models consistently get five-star praise year after year, and which ones show repeated complaints about wobbly platforms, weak straps, or squeaky seats. We also cross-check against a handful of published expert reviews from established outdoor publications. What actually breaks on a tree stand? The climbing sticks’ welds, the strap buckles that rust, the seat that tears after a season. We note those failure points in our research. Our picks are the ones that have the least number of long-term complaints, not just the newest or most advertised models. We update this list when enough new feedback suggests a change is needed, not on a arbitrary schedule.
